Haaland is banned

Nobody owns him. Not for a week, not for a differential, not because he's on a hat-trick.

The site checks every squad after every deadline, so there's no getting away with it.

Finishing bottom

Last place doesn't pay the £20 — which is the good news, and where the good news ends.

Instead they pay for themselves to be a mascot at a football match. Everyone else comes along and makes a day of it.

How the money is worked out

Nobody records anything by hand. The site reads Fantasy Premier League directly every couple of hours and recalculates the whole season from scratch each time.

That means no double-charging, no forgetting, and no debate. Every fee and fine is listed with a date on the main page under Every penny.

Disputes go to the ledger, not the group chat.
